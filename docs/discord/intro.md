Introducing **Veil Privacy Protocol (VPP)** — private stablecoins on Solana.

Veil is a non-custodial protocol that lets users wrap normal stablecoins like USDC and USDT into **vAssets** such as vUSDC and vUSDT. vAssets are designed to hide balances and transfer amounts while staying 1:1 backed and fully redeemable.

The goal is simple: users and apps should not have to choose between fast stablecoin payments and financial privacy.

Veil is infrastructure, not a company product. Wallets, payment apps, DeFi protocols, and projects like TrustLink Pay will be able to integrate protected stablecoin balances through the Veil SDK.

Created by **Agbaka Daniel Ugonna (Big Dreams Web3)**.

Early build is starting now: vault program, Token-2022 vAsset design, SDK interfaces, and protocol docs.
